FAQ

Everything owners often ask before choosing short- or long-term management.

Tip: Try “tax”, “license”, “maintenance”, “SEF”.
How is rental income taxed in Portugal? +
Tax treatment depends on your situation (individual vs company), property type, and deductions you qualify for. We help you stay organized with the reporting that usually comes with guest stays, but you should confirm the final tax calculation with a qualified accountant.
Do I need a license for short-term rentals (AL)? +
In many cases, short-term rentals require a Local Accommodation registration/license (often referred to as “AL”). Requirements can vary by municipality and can change over time. We can guide you on what applies to your property and help manage the operations, while you confirm legal requirements with the right local professionals.
What about SEF/guest reporting and tourist tax? +
Depending on the current rules, guest data reporting and tourist tax processes must be handled correctly. As part of our management, we make sure the required steps are handled so you have peace of mind—again, final responsibility and tax specifics should be verified with your advisor.
How do you handle maintenance and repairs? +
We use a structured approach: preventive check-ins, clear cleaning coordination, and fast response when issues happen. For minor repairs and urgent items, we coordinate with trusted local professionals and document everything so your property remains in excellent condition.
Who manages utilities and ongoing costs? +
It depends on your agreement. In our premium options, we can manage utilities (water/electric/internet coordination) and keep costs predictable. We also plan around recurring monthly expenses so owners understand the real net outcome.
